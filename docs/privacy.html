Most privacy pages are vibes. This one is grep commands. Every claim below is something you can check in less than sixty seconds.

#The short version

Lyra has two transcription modes, and they have very different privacy properties.

Cloud / Custom API (the default) uploads your recorded audio to the provider you configure — OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or your own local gateway. If AI post-processing or Smart Voice Editing is on, the transcribed text (and any text you had selected) also goes to the chat endpoint you configured. Once data reaches that provider, their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Local Whisper runs entirely on your Mac through whisper.cpp. Select it, turn off AI post-processing and Smart Voice Editing, and nothing leaves the machine. That is the configuration that is genuinely 100% local.

In both modes there is no Lyra server, no telemetry, no account and no update pings. Every outbound connection goes to an endpoint you configured, or to HuggingFace for a model file you asked for.

#What we never do

  • ✗ Run a server of our own — your data never passes through Lyra infrastructure
  • ✗ Track you — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports
  • ✗ Phone home — no update server, no license check, no pings
  • ✗ Write your audio to disk — recordings live in RAM and are discarded
  • ✗ Store your API key in plaintext — it lives in the macOS Keychain
  • ✗ Send your key to a provider you didn't enter it for — speech and AI endpoints are keyed separately
  • ✗ Download models behind your back — cloud-only users get no silent HuggingFace pulls
  • ✗ Load web fonts or remote scripts — the app is 100% native

#What we do do — and where it goes

  • → Upload audio in Cloud/API mode — to the Base URL you configured
  • → Upload draft text when AI post-processing is on — to the AI Text Provider you configured
  • → Upload your selection when Smart Voice Editing rewrites highlighted text — same endpoint
  • → Use the clipboard for the default paste-based insertion — we snapshot, paste and restore it; switch to "Simulate Keystrokes" to opt out
  • → Save dictation history to ~/Library/Application Support/Lyra/history.json — unencrypted JSON; turn it off with Private mode in Advanced settings
  • → Fetch model files from HuggingFace — when you click Download, or when Local Whisper is selected and no model is present

#Permission receipt

Here's exactly what the app does with the permissions it asks for:

Microphone

  • Captures audio only during an active dictation session
  • Buffer stays in RAM
  • Converted to 16kHz mono
  • Sent to your provider, or to local Whisper
  • Discarded after transcription; never written to disk

Accessibility

  • Listens for your hotkey (CGEvent tap)
  • Types or pastes transcribed text at the cursor
  • Reads the current selection only when Smart Voice Editing is on
  • Does not read screen contents
  • Does not log or transmit what you type

#Build it yourself

Paranoid? We respect that. You never have to trust the DMG on our releases page. Clone the repo, run make whisper && make app, and you'll have a binary built from source you can read line by line.

Want to be really sure? Switch to Local Whisper, turn off AI post-processing and Smart Voice Editing, then run Little Snitch or LuLu while you dictate. You'll see zero outbound connections. Leave Cloud mode on instead, and you'll see exactly one destination: the provider whose Base URL you typed in yourself.
